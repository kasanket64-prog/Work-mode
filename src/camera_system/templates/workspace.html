BEFORE YOU START

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Know what to expect
from your first analysis.

Do I need to connect a camera?

No. Start by uploading a video or image. Live camera and RTSP streams are available through the project's `camera-video` CLI tool; this website focuses on uploaded recordings.

Where does my footage go?

This version processes footage on your computer. Uploads and results stay in the local workspace until you remove them from disk. It does not publish recordings or create a public share link.

Does it analyze the entire recording?

Videos are sampled at your chosen stride, up to your selected sample limit. Check Coverage & processing details for each run. A completed analysis does not mean every frame or the entire video was analyzed.

How reliable are the findings?

Findings are model predictions and rule-based event candidates, not verified facts. Review the source evidence before acting. Real-world accuracy has not yet been measured on external footage.

How long will it take?

It depends on your hardware, the footage, and the sample limit. When `--warm-worker` is active, loaded weights stay warm across consecutive runs. Start with Quick look; you can stop a running job anytime.

What can I export?

Download structured JSONL session results from the workspace. Inside the review studio, verdicts save directly to SQLite, Export JSON downloads full session output, and reviewed annotations can be evaluated live in browser.
