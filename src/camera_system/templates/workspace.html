BEFORE YOU START

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Know what to expect
from your first analysis.

Do I need to connect a camera?

No. Start by uploading a video or image. Live camera input is available through the project's command-line tools; this website focuses on uploaded recordings.

Where does my footage go?

This version processes footage on your computer. Uploads and results stay in the local workspace until you remove them from disk. It does not publish recordings or create a public share link.

Does it analyze the entire recording?

Videos are sampled every 15 frames, up to your selected sample limit. Check Coverage & processing details for each run. A completed analysis does not mean every frame or the entire video was analyzed.

How reliable are the findings?

Findings are model predictions and rule-based event candidates, not verified facts. Review the source evidence before acting. Real-world accuracy has not yet been measured.

How long will it take?

It depends on your computer, the footage and the sample limit. The first run may download and load models. Start with Quick look; you can stop a running analysis from the workspace.

What can I export?

Download structured analysis results from the workspace. In the review, Export JSON saves your event verdicts too. Reviewed video annotations can also be used to evaluate predictions.
